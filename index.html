What the app does

Weighted Canvas categories, what if scores, and optional alerts when a grade changes.

Weighted categories

Canvas assignment groups, including groups with zero weight that don’t count toward the total.

What if scores

Plug in a score you don’t have yet and see the new course percent.

Grade alerts

Optional. After a sync, notify you if a score showed up or changed.

School search

Search your campus by name and see if Canvas sync is supported there.

Canvas sign in

If your campus is supported, you sign in with Canvas. We never ask for your password.

On device

Courses and grades stay on the phone. No student accounts. We don’t sell transcripts.
